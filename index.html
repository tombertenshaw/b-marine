Services

What B-Marine delivers

Projects across the full spectrum of naval architecture and marine engineering — new builds, existing vessels, and technical engineering deliverables.

Custom Vessel Design

New build design for recreational yachts and motor vessels. Hull form development, general arrangement, structural design, and construction documentation tailored to the build method and client's operating profile.

Commercial Vessel Design

Design and engineering for workboats, charter vessels, fishing craft, and other commercial applications. Compliance with Maritime New Zealand requirements and applicable classification rules where required.

Retrofits & Upgrades

Engineering assessment and design for modifications to existing vessels. Structural alterations, machinery changes, deck equipment, and system upgrades — with full consideration of the effect on the vessel as a whole.

Structural Engineering

Scantling design and structural analysis for hulls, decks, bulkheads, and major structural members. First-principles calculations and rule-based approaches, documented for survey or client use.

Stability Analysis

Intact and damage stability assessment for new and existing vessels. Inclining experiments, lightship surveys, loading conditions, and stability booklet preparation to applicable standards.

"Engineering that holds up under scrutiny — on paper and on the water."
